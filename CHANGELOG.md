## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-18 — Beta

Initial public beta.

### Added

- Artifact multipliers for tower crosspaths, hero levels, and battle conditions, with expandable adjusted stats.
- Build comparisons and previews, a three-checkpoint run planner, saved builds/plans, share links, JSON transfers, and undo/redo.
- Reference sources, evidence, modeled effects, and calculation exclusions.

### Known limitations

- 80 of 117 artifacts affect calculations; the remainder are reference-only, and some modeled effects are partial.
- DPS estimates cover one selected attack component, not total tower or party damage. Hit eligibility remains unknown except for explicit component exclusions.
- Ability timing and uncertain mechanics remain scoped estimates with documented restrictions. Costs, survival, and curse-aware feasibility are excluded.
- Community data snapshots may differ from the current game.
