Artifact effect coverage

Modeled effects remain estimates. “Verified export cooldown” verifies one matching v56.0 source value only. It does not verify an entire artifact or current-game behavior. Critical hits are optional description estimates with unverified stacking. Unsupported effects do not change totals.

Prepared tests & evidence requirements
  • Crit stacking: baseline, each artifact alone, both acquisition orders, per-hit observations and repeated trials.
  • Cooldown floors: baseline and increasing modifier combinations, timestamped attack/ability intervals, separate frame precision from a possible floor.
  • Ambiguous effects: one target, condition or artifact change per trial, with explicit unknowns for unavailable controls.

Record game version, platform, mode, exact tower paths or hero level, artifacts/rarities, curses, buffs, target, placement, raw measurements and recordings. Calculator estimates and pinned export values are hypotheses, never observed results.

    Explain this calculation

      Damage steps end in damage per hit. The last two steps convert this to single-target DPS. Pierce and range are independent.

      Accuracy & excluded effects

      Ability timings are nominal estimates that assume cooldown starts on activation and the ability is reused immediately. Charges, cooldown floors, target availability, round boundaries and activation delays can add downtime. Ability damage is excluded.

      Multipliers stack multiplicatively. Boss damage includes MOAB bonuses. Attack rate uses compatible verified cooldowns where available, otherwise rounded descriptions. DPS counts one hit per attack cycle for the selected component, even when it fires several projectiles. Pierce and range are tracked separately and do not multiply this single-target DPS estimate. Expected artifact crits use described probabilities. Innate tower crits, abilities, other projectiles, and boss mechanics remain excluded. Target flags select damage bonuses, not detection or immunities. Frozen, glued, and stunned target bonuses affect regular damage only.

      “Verified export cooldown” means a matching pinned source value, not verified current-game behavior. Critical estimates are optional. Overlapping crit mechanics are excluded.

        This is stat-based planning. Detection and damage-type coverage, curses, starting-artifact restrictions, economy/costs, placement, map geometry, wave density, projectile travel, uptime, DOT, extra attacks, and ability damage are not simulated.
